# E-commerce UX Patterns

E-commerce products must build trust, reduce friction, and guide users from discovery to purchase. Small UX improvements can have a large impact on conversion and revenue.

---

## 1. Core E-commerce Principles

- **Reduce friction at every step.** Fewer taps, clearer choices, faster checkout.
- **Build trust.** Reviews, security badges, clear policies, and transparent pricing.
- **Show the right information at the right time.** Price, availability, shipping, and returns should be easy to find.
- **Support browsing and searching.** Different users shop differently.
- **Make checkout feel safe and easy.** Minimize required fields and surprises.

---

## 2. Product Discovery

### Search

- Prominent search bar.
- Autocomplete and typo tolerance.
- Filters and sorting.
- Recent and popular searches.

### Category navigation

- Clear category hierarchy.
- Mega menus for large catalogs.
- Breadcrumbs for orientation.
- Faceted filters for narrowing.

### Recommendations

- Personalized suggestions.
- Related and complementary products.
- Recently viewed.
- Trending and best sellers.

---

## 3. Product Detail Pages

A product page should answer every question before the user asks it.

### Essential elements

- Clear product name and high-quality images.
- Price, availability, and shipping information.
- Size, color, and configuration options.
- Add to cart and buy now buttons.
- Detailed description and specifications.
- Customer reviews and ratings.
- Return and shipping policies.
- Trust signals and security badges.

### Best practices

- Use multiple images and zoom.
- Show stock status honestly.
- Surface delivery estimates early.
- Make CTA prominent and sticky on mobile.
- Avoid hiding important info behind tabs.

---

## 4. Cart and Checkout

### Cart

- Show item images, names, quantities, and prices.
- Allow quantity updates and removal.
- Display savings, shipping, and taxes.
- Provide clear “Checkout” and “Continue shopping” actions.
- Save cart for later or across devices.

### Checkout

- Minimize steps and fields.
- Offer guest checkout.
- Use autofill-friendly fields.
- Show progress indicators.
- Provide multiple payment options.
- Confirm order details before final submit.
- Show order confirmation and tracking info.

### Best practices

- Do not surprise users with hidden fees.
- Use inline validation on blur.
- Make error messages specific and helpful.
- Support one-click or wallet payments.
- Show security and privacy assurances.

---

## 5. Trust and Social Proof

- Customer reviews and ratings.
- User-generated photos and videos.
- Best-seller and trending badges.
- Clear return, refund, and shipping policies.
- Secure checkout symbols.
- Live chat or support links.

---

## 6. Mobile E-commerce

- Large product images.
- Sticky add-to-cart button.
- Swipeable product galleries.
- Simple, thumb-friendly filters.
- Streamlined checkout.
- Mobile payment options (Apple Pay, Google Pay).

---

## 7. Post-Purchase

- Clear order confirmation.
- Shipping and tracking updates.
- Easy access to order history.
- Simple returns and exchanges.
- Request for reviews after delivery.
- Reorder and subscription options.

---

## 8. Common E-commerce Mistakes

- Hidden costs at checkout.
- Forced account creation.
- Poor or missing search and filters.
- Slow or unclear checkout.
- Insufficient product information.
- Fake urgency or fake social proof.
- No guest checkout.
- Complex returns process.

---

## 9. Checklist

- [ ] Search is prominent and forgiving.
- [ ] Navigation and filters help users find products.
- [ ] Product pages answer all key questions.
- [ ] Prices, shipping, and policies are transparent.
- [ ] Cart shows clear totals and actions.
- [ ] Checkout is fast, simple, and secure.
- [ ] Guest checkout is available.
- [ ] Mobile experience is optimized.
- [ ] Trust and social proof are visible.
- [ ] Post-purchase communication is clear.
