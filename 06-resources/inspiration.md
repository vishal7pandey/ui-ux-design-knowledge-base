## How to Use Inspiration

### Do
- **Study patterns** — understand why a design works, not just how it looks
- **Take screenshots** — build a personal reference library
- **Analyze structure** — look at the grid, spacing, hierarchy
- **Adapt, don't copy** — learn from the approach, create your own solution
- **Save references** — use tools like Eagle, Milanote, or a simple folder

### Don't
- **Don't copy directly** — your context is different from theirs
- **Don't follow trends blindly** — what works for them may not work for you
- **Don't skip the thinking** — inspiration without understanding leads to superficial design
- **Don't forget your users** — what looks good may not work for your audience
- **Don't steal** — respect copyrights and licenses
