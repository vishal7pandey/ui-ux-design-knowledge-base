# AI Search and Retrieval UX

AI-powered search helps users find and make sense of information faster. It combines traditional search with natural language understanding, synthesis, and answers.

---

## 1. Key UX Principles

### Natural language first

- Users can ask questions, not just type keywords.
- Understand intent and context.

### Answer and source

- Provide a direct answer when possible.
- Always show sources and let users verify.

### Results and synthesis

- Combine multiple sources into a clear response.
- Link to the original documents.

### Control

- Let users refine, filter, and explore.
- Support traditional search operators and filters.

### Transparency

- Show how the search works.
- Explain when the answer is uncertain.

---

## 2. Search Patterns

### Conversational search

- The user asks in natural language.
- The AI responds with an answer and sources.
- Follow-up questions keep the context.

### Answer box

- A concise answer at the top of results.
- Followed by links and related questions.

### Document Q&A

- The user asks questions about uploaded or stored documents.
- The AI answers based on those documents.

### Semantic search

- Search by meaning, not just keyword.
- Find relevant content even with different wording.

### Faceted and filtered search

- Combine AI answers with filters.
- Let users narrow results by date, type, source, etc.

---

## 3. Results Presentation

### Summarized answer

- A brief, synthesized answer at the top.
- Use bullets, lists, and tables when helpful.

### Source links

- Cite the documents or pages used.
- Link to the relevant section.

### Excerpt snippets

- Show the key passage from each source.
- Help users scan and verify.

### Related queries

- Suggest follow-up questions.
- Help users explore the topic.

### “No results” with help

- Suggest rephrasing or alternate terms.
- Show similar or broader results.

---

## 4. Trust and Verification

- Show which sources were used.
- Indicate confidence and coverage.
- Highlight uncertain or incomplete answers.
- Provide the original document for manual review.

---

## 5. Best Practices

- Make search fast and responsive.
- Support both natural language and keyword search.
- Provide direct answers and source links.
- Let users filter and refine.
- Handle ambiguous queries by asking clarifying questions.
- Show progress when AI is thinking.
- Keep the interface scannable.
- Test with real user queries.

---

## 6. Common Mistakes

- Hiding sources behind an answer.
- Over-synthesizing and losing nuance.
- Hallucinating answers without a source.
- No way to refine or filter results.
- Slow, unresponsive search.
- Not handling ambiguous or empty queries.
- Treating AI search as a black box.
- No support for follow-up questions.

---

## 7. Checklist

- [ ] Natural language and keyword search are supported.
- [ ] Answers are synthesized with source links.
- [ ] Sources are easy to verify.
- [ ] Confidence and uncertainty are shown.
- [ ] Filters and facets are available.
- [ ] Follow-up questions are supported.
- [ ] Empty or ambiguous queries are handled.
- [ ] Search is fast and responsive.
- [ ] Results are scannable and useful.
- [ ] Search is tested with real user queries.
