# AI in Customer Data and CRM UX

AI in customer data and CRM helps businesses understand, engage, and retain customers. The UX must be ethical, accurate, and respectful of privacy.

---

## 1. Key Principles

### Single customer view

- Unify data from many touchpoints.
- Show a clear, complete picture of the customer.

### Actionable insights

- Turn data into next-best actions.
- Help sales, support, and marketing teams act.

### Privacy

- Customer data is sensitive.
- Use consent and protect data.
- Be transparent.

### Accuracy

- Customer profiles and predictions must be correct.
- Avoid stale or merged records.

### Ethics

- Do not use data to manipulate or discriminate.
- Provide value to the customer, not just the business.

---

## 2. Common Use Cases

- Customer segmentation.
- Lead scoring.
- Churn prediction.
- Next best action.
- Sentiment analysis.
- Case and opportunity summaries.
- Personalization.
- Customer journey mapping.
- Lifetime value prediction.

---

## 3. UX Patterns

### Customer 360 view

- Show profile, history, interactions, and predicted needs.
- Use a clean, scannable layout.
- Link to source records.

### Lead scoring

- Show score and factors.
- Explain why a lead is hot or cold.
- Let sales adjust and override.

### Churn risk

- Flag customers at risk.
- Show reasons and suggested actions.
- Track outcomes.

### Next best action

- Recommend the best next step.
- “Call this customer today.”
- “Send a renewal offer.”
- Explain the recommendation.

### Conversation summary

- Summarize emails, calls, and tickets.
- Show key points and next steps.
- Keep data private.

### Opportunity insights

- Predict win probability.
- Identify blockers and influencers.
- Suggest follow-ups.

### Customer journey

- Visualize touchpoints over time.
- Identify friction and opportunities.
- Show engagement trends.

---

## 4. Best Practices

- Keep customer data accurate and up to date.
- Provide source and confidence for predictions.
- Let users correct and enrich profiles.
- Make next-best actions easy to execute.
- Integrate with email, chat, and support tools.
- Respect consent and privacy.
- Avoid creepy or invasive personalization.
- Test for bias in segmentation and scoring.
- Provide clear data retention and deletion.
- Train users on AI features.

---

## 5. Privacy and Ethics

- Show what data is used.
- Provide opt-out and deletion.
- Avoid using sensitive attributes for scoring.
- Do not sell or misuse customer data.
- Be transparent with customers.

---

## 6. Common Mistakes

- Inaccurate or duplicate customer records.
- Creepy or irrelevant recommendations.
- Scoring based on biased data.
- No way to override AI.
- Not respecting privacy.
- Overloading users with alerts.
- Data silos preventing a single view.
- No explanation for predictions.

---

## 7. Checklist

- [ ] Customer profiles are unified and accurate.
- [ ] Predictions show confidence and reasoning.
- [ ] Lead and churn scores are explainable.
- [ ] Next-best actions are clear and easy to execute.
- [ ] Conversation summaries are useful and private.
- [ ] Customer journeys are visual and actionable.
- [ ] Privacy and consent are respected.
- [ ] Users can override and correct AI.
- [ ] Bias is tested and mitigated.
- [ ] Data is integrated across channels.
- [ ] Training and onboarding are provided.
- [ ] The product is tested with sales, support, and marketing teams.
