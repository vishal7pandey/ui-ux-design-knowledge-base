# AI in News and Journalism UX

AI can help gather, write, fact-check, and distribute news. The UX must prioritize accuracy, transparency, and trust in an environment where misinformation spreads quickly.

---

## 1. Key Principles

### Accuracy

- News must be factual and verified.
- AI should not fabricate quotes, events, or sources.
- Human journalists review AI output.

### Transparency

- Clearly label AI-generated or AI-assisted content.
- Explain how AI was used.
- Show sources and methods.

### Trust

- Readers must trust the publication.
- AI should support, not erode, credibility.

### Editorial control

- Human editors make final decisions.
- AI can assist but not replace journalistic judgment.

### Bias and fairness

- Monitor for bias in story selection and language.
- Diversify sources and perspectives.

---

## 2. Common Use Cases

- Trend detection and story discovery.
- Draft generation for breaking news.
- Summarization of reports and press releases.
- Transcription and translation.
- Fact-checking and source verification.
- Content personalization.
- Headline and snippet generation.
- Automated data journalism.

---

## 3. UX Patterns

### Story discovery

- Surface trending topics and potential stories.
- Show why a topic is being flagged.
- Let journalists investigate before writing.

### Draft assistance

- Generate a first draft from notes or data.
- Journalist edits and verifies.
- Track what the AI wrote vs. the journalist.

### Fact-checking

- Verify claims against sources.
- Highlight uncertain or unsupported statements.
- Link to evidence.

### Summarization

- Summarize long documents or press releases.
- Preserve nuance and context.
- Link to the original source.

### Transcription

- Convert audio or video to text.
- Identify speakers.
- Let journalists edit and correct.

### Translation

- Translate articles for global audiences.
- Preserve tone and accuracy.
- Human review for sensitive content.

### Content labeling

- “AI-assisted reporting.”
- “This article was generated or summarized with AI and reviewed by an editor.”
- Be clear and specific.

### Reader trust signals

- Show bylines, editorial notes, and source links.
- Provide model or method disclosure.
- Allow readers to report errors.

---

## 4. Best Practices

- Fact-check AI output before publishing.
- Attribute all sources.
- Avoid anonymous or hallucinated quotes.
- Label AI involvement clearly.
- Keep human editors in the loop.
- Monitor for bias and stereotypes.
- Protect source confidentiality.
- Provide corrections and updates clearly.
- Respect copyright and fair use.
- Test with journalists and readers.

---

## 5. Ethics and Accountability

- Do not use AI to create misleading or clickbait content.
- Disclose conflicts of interest.
- Provide correction and appeal processes.
- Maintain editorial independence from AI vendors.
- Document AI training data and limitations.

---

## 6. Common Mistakes

- Publishing AI-generated content without verification.
- Fabricated quotes or sources.
- Inaccurate summarization.
- No disclosure of AI use.
- Bias in story selection or language.
- Replacing journalists with AI.
- Infringing copyright.
- Not correcting errors.

---

## 7. Checklist

- [ ] AI output is fact-checked and verified.
- [ ] Sources are cited and linked.
- [ ] AI involvement is clearly labeled.
- [ ] Human editors review before publishing.
- [ ] Bias and stereotypes are monitored.
- [ ] Summaries preserve nuance.
- [ ] Translations are accurate and reviewed.
- [ ] Reader trust signals are present.
- [ ] Corrections are easy to make and display.
- [ ] Source confidentiality is protected.
- [ ] Copyright and fair use are respected.
- [ ] The product is tested with journalists and readers.
