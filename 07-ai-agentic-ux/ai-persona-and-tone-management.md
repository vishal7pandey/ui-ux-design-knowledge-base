# AI Persona and Tone Management

An AI’s persona and tone shape how users feel about the product. Managing them well means consistency, clarity, and alignment with user expectations.

---

## 1. Persona vs. Tone

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Persona** | The overall character or identity of the AI. |
| **Tone** | The emotional quality of the AI’s messages in a given context. |

A persona is stable. Tone can shift depending on the situation.

---

## 2. Defining the AI Persona

### Core traits

- Friendly but professional.
- Helpful and concise.
- Honest about limits.
- Not overly apologetic or servile.

### Name and identity

- Use a name, but avoid implying humanity.
- Provide a clear visual marker or icon.
- Do not use a real-sounding human photo.

### Role

- Define what the AI does.
- Define what it does not do.
- Set boundaries for autonomy.

---

## 3. Tone Dimensions

| Dimension | Examples |
|-----------|----------|
| **Formality** | Casual, neutral, formal. |
| **Enthusiasm** | Energetic, calm, serious. |
| **Confidence** | Cautious, direct, tentative. |
| **Empathy** | Warm, neutral, business-like. |
| **Verbosity** | Brief, detailed, balanced. |

---

## 4. Tone by Context

### Onboarding

- Friendly and encouraging.
- Use simple, inviting language.

### Errors

- Calm and helpful.
- No blame. Clear next steps.

### High-stakes output

- Cautious and precise.
- Use qualifiers and sources.

### Everyday assistance

- Efficient and clear.
- Get to the point.

---

## 5. Managing Tone

### Tone settings

- Let users choose a preferred tone where appropriate.
- Options: concise, detailed, casual, formal.
- Apply consistently across responses.

### Brand alignment

- The AI tone should match the product’s brand voice.
- Document tone guidelines in a style guide.

### Testing and iteration

- Test different tones with users.
- Measure satisfaction and task success.
- Refine based on feedback.

### Tone governance

- Define who can change the AI tone.
- Keep tone consistent across teams and features.
- Avoid drift over time.

---

## 6. Avoiding Over-Personality

- Do not let the personality overshadow the task.
- Avoid jokes, emojis, or slang unless on-brand.
- Do not pretend to have feelings.
- Keep the focus on helping the user.

---

## 7. Common Mistakes

- Inconsistent tone across the product.
- Persona that is too human-like.
- Tone that does not match the brand.
- Overusing personality in serious contexts.
- Not letting users control tone.
- Tone that makes the AI seem overconfident.
- No documentation for the AI voice.

---

## 8. Checklist

- [ ] AI persona is defined and documented.
- [ ] Tone dimensions are mapped to contexts.
- [ ] Persona is consistent across features.
- [ ] Tone matches the product brand.
- [ ] Users can adjust tone where useful.
- [ ] Tone is appropriate for the task and emotional context.
- [ ] AI does not pretend to be human.
- [ ] Personality does not overshadow usefulness.
- [ ] Tone is tested with users.
- [ ] Tone guidelines are in the design or content style guide.
