# AI in Sales and Revenue UX

AI in sales and revenue helps teams identify opportunities, prioritize leads, forecast, and close deals. The UX should be actionable, transparent, and motivating.

---

## 1. Key Principles

### Actionable

- AI should tell salespeople what to do next.
- Every insight should lead to a clear action.

### Trust

- Salespeople need to trust lead scores, forecasts, and recommendations.
- Show reasoning and data.

### Control

- Salespeople should be able to override and adjust.
- AI supports, not replaces, the sales process.

### Motivation

- Use gamification and recognition thoughtfully.
- Celebrate wins and progress.

### Accuracy

- Forecasts and predictions should be reliable.
- Avoid overconfidence.

---

## 2. Common Use Cases

- Lead and opportunity scoring.
- Sales forecasting.
- Pipeline management.
- Next best action.
- Email and call coaching.
- Pricing and quote optimization.
- Account planning.
- Territory management.
- Revenue intelligence.

---

## 3. UX Patterns

### Pipeline view

- Show deals by stage, value, and win probability.
- Highlight deals that need attention.
- Allow drag and drop.

### Lead scoring

- Show score and factors.
- Explain why a lead is hot.
- Let sales adjust.

### Opportunity score

- Predict win probability.
- Show key drivers.
- Suggest next steps.

### Forecast dashboard

- Show predicted revenue.
- Compare to quota and history.
- Show confidence and assumptions.

### Next best action

- “Call this prospect.”
- “Send a follow-up email.”
- “Schedule a demo.”
- Explain the recommendation.

### Email and call coaching

- Analyze communication.
- Suggest improvements.
- Provide templates and talking points.

### Quote and pricing

- Recommend pricing and discounts.
- Show margin and approval thresholds.
- Let sales adjust and get approval.

### Account plan

- Identify growth opportunities.
- Show key contacts, risks, and open opportunities.
- Suggest actions.

---

## 4. Best Practices

- Integrate with CRM, email, and calendar.
- Keep data accurate and updated.
- Provide clear reasons for scores and forecasts.
- Let salespeople override AI.
- Avoid micromanagement.
- Support mobile for field sales.
- Protect customer and deal data.
- Test for bias in scoring.
- Provide training on AI insights.
- Use simple, motivating language.

---

## 5. Trust and Ethics

- Do not manipulate salespeople or customers.
- Avoid biased lead scoring.
- Be transparent about how forecasts are made.
- Respect customer data and consent.

---

## 6. Common Mistakes

- Black-box scores and forecasts.
- Too many low-value alerts.
- Not integrating with CRM.
- Over-reliance on AI for forecasts.
- No way to override.
- Biased lead scoring.
- Inaccurate data.
- Not updating as deals progress.

---

## 7. Checklist

- [ ] Pipeline shows value, probability, and next steps.
- [ ] Lead and opportunity scores are explainable.
- [ ] Forecasts include confidence and assumptions.
- [ ] Next-best actions are clear and actionable.
- [ ] Coaching is useful, not intrusive.
- [ ] Pricing and quoting are accurate and approved.
- [ ] Account plans identify growth and risk.
- [ ] CRM, email, and calendar are integrated.
- [ ] Salespeople can override AI.
- [ ] Mobile access is supported.
- [ ] Data is protected and accurate.
- [ ] Bias and fairness are tested.
- [ ] The product is tested with sales teams.
