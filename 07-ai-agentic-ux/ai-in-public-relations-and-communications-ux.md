# AI in Public Relations and Communications UX

AI in public relations and communications helps teams monitor, craft, and distribute messages. The UX should protect reputation, ensure accuracy, and support human judgment.

---

## 1. Key Principles

### Accuracy

- Communications must be factual.
- AI should not invent quotes, data, or sources.
- Verify before sending.

### Brand and tone

- Messages must match brand voice.
- Maintain consistency across channels.

### Speed and timeliness

- PR often responds to fast-moving events.
- AI should help draft quickly without sacrificing care.

### Reputation protection

- Monitor for crises and negative sentiment.
- Provide early warnings and response guidance.

### Transparency

- Disclose AI use where appropriate.
- Be honest about limitations.

---

## 2. Common Use Cases

- Media monitoring and sentiment analysis.
- Press release drafting.
- Crisis communication support.
- Message testing and optimization.
- Stakeholder segmentation.
- Social listening.
- Reporting and coverage analysis.
- Spokesperson preparation.
- Internal communications.

---

## 3. UX Patterns

### Media monitoring dashboard

- Track mentions, sentiment, and reach.
- Filter by topic, channel, and time.
- Alert to spikes and trends.

### Sentiment analysis

- Show overall and topic-level sentiment.
- Highlight shifts and potential issues.
- Let users review and adjust classification.

### Press release assistant

- Generate drafts from key facts.
- Suggest structure and quotes.
- Require human review and approval.

### Crisis center

- Monitor emerging issues.
- Suggest response messages.
- Track team tasks and deadlines.

### Message testing

- Test different messages with sample audiences.
- Show predicted sentiment and engagement.
- Compare options.

### Coverage report

- Summarize media coverage.
- Show key themes, reach, and sentiment.
- Export for stakeholders.

### Stakeholder mapping

- Identify key audiences and influencers.
- Show interests and relationships.
- Suggest engagement strategies.

### Spokesperson prep

- Generate Q&A from likely questions.
- Provide talking points.
- Allow review and customization.

---

## 4. Best Practices

- Require human approval for public statements.
- Cite sources and verify claims.
- Monitor for misinformation and negative sentiment.
- Support multi-channel communication.
- Maintain brand voice and consistency.
- Provide clear crisis workflows.
- Protect sensitive information.
- Test messages before wide release.
- Keep AI use transparent.
- Train communicators on AI tools.

---

## 5. Ethics and Trust

- Do not use AI to generate misleading content.
- Avoid astroturfing or fake engagement.
- Respect embargoes and confidentiality.
- Disclose AI involvement when relevant.
- Protect journalists and sources.

---

## 6. Common Mistakes

- Publishing AI-drafted statements without review.
- Hallucinated quotes or facts.
- Overlooking negative sentiment.
- Inconsistent tone across channels.
- No crisis escalation path.
- Misleading attribution or data.
- Ignoring stakeholder concerns.
- Poor timing of automated responses.

---

## 7. Checklist

- [ ] Media monitoring is comprehensive and real-time.
- [ ] Sentiment is tracked and alerts are clear.
- [ ] Press releases are drafted but human-approved.
- [ ] Crisis workflows are supported.
- [ ] Messages are tested before release.
- [ ] Coverage reports are accurate and useful.
- [ ] Stakeholders and influencers are mapped.
- [ ] Spokesperson prep is customizable.
- [ ] Brand voice is consistent.
- [ ] Sources are verified and cited.
- [ ] AI use is disclosed when appropriate.
- [ ] The product is tested with communications teams.
