# AI in HR and Recruiting UX

AI in HR and recruiting helps find, screen, and support candidates and employees. The UX must be fair, transparent, and respectful of human dignity.

---

## 1. Key Principles

### Fairness

- AI should not discriminate.
- Monitor for bias in screening and recommendations.
- Include diverse candidates.

### Transparency

- Candidates and employees should know when AI is used.
- Explain how decisions are made.
- Provide feedback and appeal paths.

### Human oversight

- Final hiring decisions should involve humans.
- AI can assist, not replace, recruiters and managers.

### Privacy

- Protect candidate and employee data.
- Be clear about data use and retention.
- Allow data deletion where required.

### Candidate experience

- Make applications easy and respectful.
- Communicate status and next steps.
- Avoid ghosting or one-way interactions.

---

## 2. Common Use Cases

- Resume screening.
- Candidate sourcing.
- Interview scheduling.
- Skills assessment.
- Job matching.
- Employee feedback and engagement.
- Onboarding support.
- Performance and development.

---

## 3. UX Patterns

### Job matching

- Match candidates to jobs based on skills and experience.
- Show why the job is a match.
- Let candidates adjust their profile.

### Resume screening

- Highlight relevant skills and gaps.
- Explain the match score.
- Let recruiters review and override.

### Interview scheduling

- Suggest times that work for everyone.
- Send invitations and reminders.
- Respect time zones and preferences.

### Skills assessment

- Use fair, job-related tests.
- Explain the purpose and process.
- Provide feedback to candidates.

### Candidate communication

- Automated status updates.
- Personalized messages.
- Clear next steps and timelines.

### Employee support

- Answer HR questions.
- Help with benefits, policies, and requests.
- Escalate sensitive issues to humans.

### Performance and development

- Suggest goals and learning based on role.
- Provide unbiased feedback.
- Support manager review.

---

## 4. Best Practices

- Audit for bias in screening and matching.
- Be transparent about AI use with candidates.
- Give candidates feedback and recourse.
- Keep humans in the loop for hiring decisions.
- Protect candidate data.
- Make applications accessible.
- Communicate clearly and promptly.
- Train recruiters and managers on AI use.
- Monitor for fairness and effectiveness.

---

## 5. Disclaimers and Messaging

- “This screening is assisted by AI. A recruiter will review all applications.”
- “Your data will be used only for this hiring process.”
- “You can request information about how your application was evaluated.”

---

## 6. Common Mistakes

- Biased algorithms that exclude qualified candidates.
- Automated rejection without human review.
- No explanation for decisions.
- Collecting unnecessary personal data.
- Impersonal or disrespectful communication.
- Ghosting candidates.
- Using AI for sensitive decisions without oversight.
- Not auditing for fairness.

---

## 7. Checklist

- [ ] AI use is disclosed to candidates.
- [ ] Screening and matching are audited for bias.
- [ ] Match scores and reasoning are explainable.
- [ ] Humans review final hiring decisions.
- [ ] Candidate data is protected.
- [ ] Communication is clear and timely.
- [ ] Feedback and appeal paths are available.
- [ ] Applications are accessible.
- [ ] Assessments are fair and job-related.
- [ ] Performance tools are unbiased.
- [ ] Regular audits test fairness and accuracy.
- [ ] Managers and recruiters are trained on AI.
