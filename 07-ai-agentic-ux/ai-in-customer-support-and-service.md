# AI in Customer Support and Service

AI can help customers get answers faster and help support teams work more efficiently. The UX must balance automation with human care.

---

## 1. Goals of AI Support

- Resolve common issues quickly.
- Reduce wait times.
- Help agents work faster and better.
- Provide 24/7 coverage.
- Escalate complex or sensitive issues to humans.

---

## 2. When to Use AI in Support

- Common questions with known answers.
- Order status, password resets, FAQs.
- Triaging and routing tickets.
- Drafting responses for agents.
- Summarizing long tickets.

When to avoid:

- Complex or emotional issues.
- High-value customers with unique needs.
- Issues requiring empathy and judgment.

---

## 3. Customer-Facing AI

### Chatbot

- Answer common questions.
- Provide links and next steps.
- Escalate to a human when needed.

### Self-service portal

- Searchable help center.
- AI-generated answers from documentation.
- Guided troubleshooting.

### Voice assistant

- Handle simple calls.
- Recognize intent and route.
- Transfer to a person for complex issues.

---

## 4. Agent-Facing AI

### Suggested replies

- Draft responses for agents to review and send.
- Reduce typing and response time.

### Ticket summarization

- Summarize long customer messages and thread history.
- Help agents get context quickly.

### Knowledge suggestions

- Suggest relevant articles or policies.
- Help agents find information faster.

### Sentiment and priority

- Detect customer frustration or urgency.
- Flag tickets for escalation.

### Coaching

- Suggest improvements to agent responses.
- Help with tone and accuracy.

---

## 5. Best Practices

- Be transparent that the customer is talking to AI.
- Make escalation easy and clear.
- Provide accurate, up-to-date information.
- Use the customer’s language and context.
- Keep the AI tone helpful and empathetic.
- Do not make customers repeat themselves.
- Use feedback to improve.
- Monitor resolution and satisfaction.
- Protect customer data and privacy.

---

## 6. Common Mistakes

- Hiding that the user is talking to a bot.
- Endless loops with no resolution.
- No way to reach a human.
- Inaccurate or outdated answers.
- Generic, unhelpful responses.
- Not understanding context from previous messages.
- Over-automating emotional or complex issues.
- Not giving agents control over AI suggestions.

---

## 7. UX Patterns

### Greeting

- “Hi, I’m an AI assistant. I can help with common questions or connect you to an agent.”

### Quick options

- Show common topics as chips.
- Reduce typing.

### Transfer

- “I’m connecting you to a specialist who can help.”
- Pass context and chat history.

### Feedback

- “Was this helpful?”
- Use to improve answers.

### Follow-up

- “Is there anything else I can help with?”
- Provide a clear next step.

---

## 8. Metrics

- First contact resolution.
- Average response time.
- Customer satisfaction (CSAT).
- Escalation rate.
- Agent handle time.
- Containment rate.
- Ticket deflection.

---

## 9. Checklist

- [ ] Customers know when they are interacting with AI.
- [ ] Common questions are answered accurately.
- [ ] Escalation to a human is easy.
- [ ] Context and history are passed to agents.
- [ ] Suggested replies are reviewed by agents.
- [ ] Tickets are summarized for agents.
- [ ] Knowledge base is kept up to date.
- [ ] Sentiment and priority are detected.
- [ ] Feedback is collected and used.
- [ ] Privacy and data are protected.
- [ ] CSAT and resolution metrics are tracked.
- [ ] Complex or emotional issues are escalated to humans.
