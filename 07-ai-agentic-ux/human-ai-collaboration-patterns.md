# Human-AI Collaboration Patterns

Human-AI collaboration is about pairing human judgment with AI capability. The right pattern depends on the task, the stakes, and the level of trust.

---

## 1. Collaboration Spectrum

| Pattern | Human role | AI role | Use case |
|---------|------------|---------|----------|
| **AI suggests, human decides** | Review and approve. | Generate options. | Recommendations, drafts. |
| **AI drafts, human edits** | Edit and finalize. | Generate a starting point. | Writing, design, code. |
| **Human steers, AI executes** | Provide direction. | Carry out tasks. | Data entry, formatting. |
| **AI monitors, human acts** | Take action on alerts. | Watch and flag. | Anomaly detection, monitoring. |
| **Human and AI co-create** | Contribute ideas. | Build and iterate. | Brainstorming, design. |
| **AI acts, human reviews** | Check and override. | Run autonomously. | Low-risk automation. |

---

## 2. Patterns

### Suggest and choose

- The AI proposes several options.
- The user picks one.
- The user can refine the choice.

### Draft and refine

- The AI creates a first version.
- The user edits, expands, or rewrites.
- Good for writing, code, and design.

### Steer and execute

- The user describes the goal.
- The AI handles the steps.
- The user can pause or redirect.

### Watch and alert

- The AI runs in the background.
- It notifies the user when something important happens.
- The user decides what to do.

### Co-create in real time

- Human and AI contribute together.
- Each can add, edit, and build on the other.
- Good for brainstorming and exploration.

### Review and approve

- The AI completes a task.
- The user reviews and approves before finalizing.
- Good for high-stakes or regulated actions.

---

## 3. Choosing the Right Pattern

### Low stakes, high trust

- AI can act with less oversight.
- Example: formatting a document.

### High stakes, low trust

- Human decides with AI suggestions.
- Example: medical, financial, legal decisions.

### Creative tasks

- AI drafts and human refines.
- Example: writing, design, code.

### Monitoring tasks

- AI watches and human acts.
- Example: security, quality control.

---

## 4. Designing for Collaboration

### Clear roles

- Make it clear who is doing what.
- Show when the AI is acting and when it is waiting.

### Easy handoff

- Make it easy for the user to take over.
- Make it easy for the AI to take over when asked.

### Undo and branch

- Let users undo or revert AI actions.
- Let users try different versions.

### Visibility

- Show what the AI is doing.
- Show why it made a suggestion.
- Show the user’s contributions.

### Credit and attribution

- Show what the AI generated.
- Show what the human changed.
- Use for review and audit.

---

## 5. Common Mistakes

- Unclear roles between human and AI.
- No way for the human to take over.
- Over-automation in high-stakes tasks.
- Hiding what the AI did.
- No undo or version history.
- Forcing collaboration when a simpler pattern would do.
- Not matching the pattern to the task and risk.

---

## 6. Checklist

- [ ] The collaboration pattern fits the task and stakes.
- [ ] Human and AI roles are clear.
- [ ] Users can take over from the AI.
- [ ] AI can take over when directed.
- [ ] Suggestions, drafts, and actions are clearly attributed.
- [ ] Undo and version history are available.
- [ ] Users can approve, reject, or edit AI output.
- [ ] The UI shows what the AI is doing and why.
- [ ] Handoffs are smooth and predictable.
- [ ] Collaboration is tested with real users.
