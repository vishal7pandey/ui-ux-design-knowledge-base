# AI in Insurance and Risk UX

AI in insurance and risk helps assess, price, and manage risk. The UX must be transparent, fair, and compliant while supporting both customers and underwriters.

---

## 1. Key Principles

### Fairness

- AI should not discriminate.
- Risk models must be audited for bias.
- Customers should be treated equitably.

### Transparency

- Explain how premiums and decisions are calculated.
- Show the factors used.
- Provide clear, understandable reasoning.

### Compliance

- Follow insurance regulations.
- Keep records for audits.
- Provide required disclosures.

### Human oversight

- High-stakes decisions need human review.
- Underwriters should be able to override AI.

### Customer trust

- Be honest about coverage, exclusions, and pricing.
- Provide easy claims and support.

---

## 2. Common Use Cases

- Underwriting and risk scoring.
- Pricing and quoting.
- Claims processing and fraud detection.
- Customer service and policy management.
- Risk assessment for businesses.
- Catastrophe and loss modeling.
- Renewal and retention.
- Compliance and reporting.

---

## 3. UX Patterns

### Quote and pricing

- Show factors that affect the price.
- Explain discounts and surcharges.
- Allow users to adjust coverage and see impact.

### Risk score explanation

- Show the risk score and what drives it.
- Use plain language.
- Provide ways to improve.

### Claims assistant

- Guide users through filing a claim.
- Extract details from photos or documents.
- Show status and next steps.

### Fraud alerts

- Flag suspicious claims.
- Provide evidence and reasoning.
- Allow human review.

### Underwriter dashboard

- Show risk, coverage, and recommendation.
- Highlight exceptions and anomalies.
- Support approval or override.

### Policy comparison

- Compare plans clearly.
- Highlight coverage, exclusions, and price.

### Renewal insight

- Explain why a premium changed.
- Suggest options.
- Allow users to review and accept.

---

## 4. Best Practices

- Use plain language for policies and pricing.
- Explain all factors that affect decisions.
- Provide clear reasons for denials or price changes.
- Allow customers to dispute or appeal.
- Protect sensitive personal and financial data.
- Audit models for bias and fairness.
- Keep humans in the loop for complex cases.
- Provide fast, empathetic claims support.
- Test for accuracy and customer understanding.
- Comply with local insurance regulations.

---

## 5. Disclaimers

- “This quote is an estimate based on the information provided.”
- “Actual premium may vary after full underwriting.”
- “AI supports, but does not replace, underwriter review.”

---

## 6. Common Mistakes

- Opaque pricing with no explanation.
- Biased risk models.
- Denying claims without clear reasons.
- No way to appeal or get human help.
- Collecting unnecessary data.
- Slow or frustrating claims process.
- Misleading coverage summaries.
- Not auditing for fairness.

---

## 7. Checklist

- [ ] Pricing factors are transparent.
- [ ] Risk scores are explainable.
- [ ] Quotes are clear and accurate.
- [ ] Claims filing is simple and guided.
- [ ] Fraud detection is fair and reviewable.
- [ ] Denials and price changes include reasons.
- [ ] Customers can appeal or get human review.
- [ ] Policies and exclusions are easy to understand.
- [ ] Data is protected and used appropriately.
- [ ] Models are audited for bias.
- [ ] Human oversight exists for complex decisions.
- [ ] Compliance with insurance regulations is maintained.
